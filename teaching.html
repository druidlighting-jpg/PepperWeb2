Doctoral Supervision

I supervise doctoral students working on questions at the intersection of business, politics, and economic policy. I'm particularly interested in students pursuing comparative or multi-country research designs.

International Experience

Beyond my permanent positions, I've held long-term visiting appointments in France, Germany, and Japan. These experiences have shaped my comparative approach to research—understanding how the same economic pressures produce different political responses in different institutional contexts.
